V26 policy update - six countermeasures

The app now uses all six countermeasures from the revised manuscript:
1. Bicyclist education and route awareness
2. Driver expectancy and response training
3. Navigation and information systems
4. Targeted enforcement and high-visibility operations
5. Shoulder rumble strips
6. Enhanced wrong-way signing at interchange ramp terminals

Cluster linkages from the revised manuscript:
- P1: Dark C1, Dark C2, Daylight C1, Daylight C2
- P2: Dark C1, Dark C2, Dark C3, Daylight C1, Daylight C2, Daylight C3
- P3: Dark C2, Dark C4, Daylight C1, Daylight C2
- P4: Dark C3, Daylight C4
- P5: Dark C1, Dark C4, Daylight C1
- P6: Dark C3, Daylight C2

The app still displays one selected countermeasure for each selected crash. Selection is constrained by the cluster linkage first, then refined using crash-record and confirmed site-review evidence.
